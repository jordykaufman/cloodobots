Card back reader test

Lay the test cards face-down and tap Scan live. Hold the phone over them, or move it slowly across the table: it reads them as you go, keeps count, and chimes when it has them all. Or take one photo of them all; zooming to 2× helps.

Tip: tilt the phone a little away from straight down so the light’s reflection misses the cards. No flash.
