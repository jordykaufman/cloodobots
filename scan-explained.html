How the envelope scan works

The phone reads each envelope card without showing it to anyone, including the person holding the card. The trick is which way everything faces.

Why a normal camera app wouldn’t work

At the table

  1. Fill the envelope as usual but don’t seal it. Take its three cards out face-down, without looking.

  2. In the app, tap Scan the envelope, then Start scanning, and lay the phone face-down on the table.

  3. Hold one card face-down about a hand’s width above the phone. Keep it still until it beeps.

  4. Do the same with the other two cards, then put all three back in the envelope and seal it.

If a card won’t read, check the room is well lit and try holding it a little higher or lower. You can always tap Let the app pick the answer instead; the game works the same, the paper envelope just isn’t used.
